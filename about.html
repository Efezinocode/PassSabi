About PassSabi AI

Your Personal AI Teacher

PassSabi AI was created to help students learn in a simple, clear, and step-by-step way. It is designed to support class assignments, homework, practice questions, and exam preparation without making learning feel stressful.

Our goal is to make learning easier by giving students a smart assistant that explains ideas, answers questions, and helps build confidence while studying.
