About PassSabi AI

Your Personal AI Teacher

PassSabi AI is an AI-powered learning platform created to help students learn any subject step by step, complete class assignments and homework, practice with quizzes, and prepare for Internal School Exams, WAEC, NECO, JAMB, GCE, NABTEB, and many other examinations.

Our mission is to make quality education simple, accessible, and available to every student through artificial intelligence.
