About PassSabi AI

Your Personal AI Teacher

PassSabi AI was created to help learners understand any subject in a simple, clear, and step-by-step way. It is designed to support class assignments, homework, practice questions, and exam preparation without making learning feel stressful.

Our goal is to make learning easier for students by giving them a smart assistant that explains ideas, answers questions, and helps them build confidence as they study.
